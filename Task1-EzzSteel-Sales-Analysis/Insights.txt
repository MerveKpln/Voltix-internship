KEY FIGURES
- Total Sales: 50,638,426,437 EGP
- Total Profit (estimated): 7,219,188,135 EGP
- Top Category: Long Steel (61.2% of total sales)
- Top Product: B500C steel grade (6.49B EGP)
- Top Region: Qalyubia (7.8% of total sales)
- Best Month: September 2023 (2.22B EGP)
- Lowest Month: August 2020 (498.5M EGP)

INSIGHTS

1. Category Concentration
Long Steel dominates the product mix, generating 61.3% of total sales 
compared to Flat Steel (HRC)'s 38.7% .

2. Top Product
SD390 is the best-selling individual product grade , closely followed
by B500C. The top 5 grades are tightly clustered, showing no single
grade overwhelmingly dominates the market.

3. Regional Distribution
Sales are geographically well-distributed: the top region (Qalyubia) accounts for
only 7.8% of total sales, with Cairo, Sharqia, and Asyut close behind - indicating
no single region drives the business disproportionately.

4. Seasonality & Growth
Revenues are volatile but trend upward from 2020 to 2023; despite seasonal dips, 
the trajectory is positive, peaking in 2023.
